## About the Project

**NIRVA AI** is a proposed unified Personal AI Workspace and Creator Marketplace designed to bring multiple AI models, persistent memory, customizable AI agents and AI product distribution into a single ecosystem.

Instead of using separate applications for different AI models, NIRVA provides a centralized workspace where users can interact with multiple AI providers, maintain personalized memory and discover specialized AI agents.

The platform also enables developers and AI creators to publish, distribute and monetize their AI models and agents through an integrated marketplace.

## Problem Statement

Current AI ecosystems have several limitations:

- AI models are distributed across different platforms.
- Users repeatedly provide the same information to different AI assistants.
- Conversations and personal context are fragmented.
- Developers face difficulties distributing and monetizing AI agents.
- Users have limited flexibility between local and cloud AI models.
- Discovering specialized AI agents and models can be difficult.

NIRVA AI aims to address these challenges through a unified and extensible AI ecosystem.

## Our Solution

NIRVA AI combines three major environments:

### 1. Personal AI Workspace

A centralized workspace for AI users.

- Multiple LLM integration.
- Unified AI chat interface.
- Persistent personal memory using Hindsight.
- Manual and automatic model selection.
- Local AI through Ollama.
- Cloud AI provider integration.
- Document upload and RAG.
- Custom AI agent installation.
- Conversation management.

### 2. Creator Studio

An environment for AI developers and creators.

- Creator registration and profiles.
- AI agent creation and publishing.
- Downloadable model registration.
- Hosted model API registration.
- Model version management.
- Product pricing configuration.
- Sales and usage analytics.
- Creator earnings management.

### 3. AI Creator Marketplace

A marketplace connecting AI creators with users.

- Discover AI models and agents.
- Search and category filtering.
- Product descriptions and ratings.
- Free and premium AI products.
- Downloadable models.
- Hosted AI model access.
- AI agent installation.
- One-time purchases.
- Monthly subscriptions.
- Pay-per-use pricing.
